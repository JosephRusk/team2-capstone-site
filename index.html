Project Overview

Executive Summary

The goal of this project is to build a drone payload with speakers, lights, and a camera. The customers are law enforcement and search-and-rescue agencies that would use the module to communicate with, locate, and assist people in need. The module will need to be small and lightweight so as to not limit the drone's effectiveness. The module will also use wireless communication with a controller to provide talk-through capabilities, light control, and live camera visuals.

Audio Communication

Onboard speakers enable two-way talk-through so operators can communicate directly with people on the ground.

Lighting

Wirelessly controlled lights help locate and signal individuals in low-visibility or nighttime conditions.

Live Camera Feed

An onboard camera streams live visuals back to the controller to support situational awareness.

Lightweight Design

Compact, low-weight construction preserves the host drone's flight performance and effectiveness.
